# The Skykeep name and logo

Skykeep's code is free software under the [GPL-3.0](LICENSE): use it, study it, change it and share it.

The name **Skykeep™** and the Skykeep logo are the project's marks. They tell people which app is the original one and where it comes from. Please don't use them in a way that could make people think something is the official Skykeep when it isn't.

**Go ahead:**

- Fork Skykeep, change it and publish it, under a different name and logo.
- Package Skykeep for a distribution or app store under the name Skykeep, without changing what the app does. Packaging fixes and integration with your distribution are fine.
- Write about Skykeep, review it, teach with it and link to it, using the name and the unmodified logo.
- Say your fork is "based on Skykeep".

**Please ask first before:**

- Using the name or logo for a modified version, a different product, or a service.
- Using the name or logo in your own product's name, domain or logo.

Questions or requests: open a [Discussion](https://github.com/YashwanthKamireddi/Skykeep/discussions).
