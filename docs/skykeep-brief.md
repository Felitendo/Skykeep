# Skykeep: product brief

**What it is:** Skykeep is a free, open-source desktop app that brings your iCloud (Notes, Reminders, Calendar, Contacts, iCloud Drive and Photos) to Linux and Windows computers as fast, native apps.

**The name:** *Sky* is the cloud, where your iCloud lives. A *keep* is the strongest, safest tower of a castle, where precious things are kept. Skykeep means **your cloud, kept safe on your own computer.**

**Who it's for:** People who use an iPhone but whose computer runs Linux or Windows. That includes everyday users who switched from a Mac and miss their notes and reminders, power users who want speed and keyboard control, and developers and privacy-minded people who want open, verifiable software.

**The problem it solves:** Apple only makes iCloud apps for its own devices. On Linux there's nothing official, and on Windows there's only a slow website or a limited app. Skykeep fixes that.

## What it offers
- **Six apps, one engine:** Skykeep Notes, Skykeep Reminders, Skykeep Calendar, Skykeep Contacts, Skykeep Drive and Skykeep Photos. Each opens as its own app, all sharing one sign-in and one sync.
- **Instant:** a copy of your iCloud lives on your computer, so every screen opens immediately, with no loading from the internet.
- **Works offline:** read and edit without a connection; changes are sent when you're back online, and nothing is lost.
- **Two-way sync:** a change on your iPhone shows up in seconds, and a change in Skykeep shows up on your iPhone.
- **Private by design:**
  - your password never leaves your computer;
  - there's no Skykeep account and no Skykeep server;
  - it talks only to Apple;
  - your sign-in is stored in your system's keyring;
  - your data stays yours.
- **Fast and native:** built in Rust, a modern, high-performance programming language, rather than as a heavy web wrapper.
- **Feels at home:** a calm, polished design, light and dark mode, system notifications with sounds and action buttons, a tray icon, quick capture for a new reminder or note from anywhere, results in your desktop's search, and opening calendar and contact files.
- **Your data, exportable:** export everything to open formats (Markdown, calendar files, contact cards) whenever you want.
- **Open source:** anyone can read the code, check the privacy claims, and contribute.

## Per app
- **Notes:** rich text, checklists, tables, pictures and drawings, folders, pinned notes, search, Markdown export.
- **Reminders:** lists and groups, smart lists, tags, sections, due dates and repeating reminders, flags and priorities, alerts with Complete and Snooze.
- **Calendar:** day, week, month and year views, drag to create and move events, repeating events, alerts, time zones, a birthdays calendar, calendar subscriptions.
- **Contacts:** every field, groups, editable addresses, import and export of contact cards.
- **Drive:** browse, upload, move, rename, quick preview, keep files for offline use.
- **Photos:** your whole library, albums, favourites, videos and Live Photos, search, download.

## Personality
Calm, trustworthy, crafted and quietly premium. Confident but never loud. Simple enough for a beginner, fast enough for an expert, open enough for a developer.
